Our Story

Simple ideas, done exceptionally well.

8055 began with a simple frustration: finding a great pair of everyday trousers was harder than it should be. Too often, comfort meant sacrificing style, and quality came with an unnecessary price tag.

So we set out to make cotton bottoms that get the fundamentals right — honest fabric, considered fits and construction that holds up wash after wash. No gimmicks, just garments you'll reach for again and again.

Today, every 8055 piece is manufactured in Turkey, working with skilled partners who share our standards for quality and responsibility.

Manufactured in Turkey

Close to the craft.

Turkey has long been a meeting point of textile tradition and modern manufacturing. It's where heritage know-how meets reliable, large-scale production — and where we chose to make every 8055 garment.

Working with experienced Turkish workshops gives us shorter supply chains, closer quality control and the flexibility to refine our fits quickly. It also means every pair carries the mark of genuine craftsmanship.
